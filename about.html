About Caden Fortenberry

Supply chain analytics manager turning operational data into measurable cost savings and better decisions.

Background

I'm Caden Fortenberry, a supply chain analytics manager. I lead analytics across logistics and supply chain optimization, using modern data platforms, automation and AI to improve how warehouse and transportation performance is measured, and to model the network decisions that reduce total landed cost.

I started on the warehouse floor as a supply chain engineer, running time studies and building labor models. That hands-on start still shapes how I work: good analytics has to reflect how operations actually run, and has to be simple enough for the people running them to use.

Over time I moved from process improvement into network-level analytics, leading labor management and continuous improvement initiatives across a nationwide warehouse network and putting dashboards and analytics tools in the hands of supply chain leaders. Today I split my time between logistics analytics and building a digital twin of the supply chain for network and route optimization scenarios.
